# Little Moments

An interactive personal photobooth for creating keepsakes and saving them to a browser-local scrapbook. Users can upload or capture photos, choose a layout, decorate a strip, download it, and revisit memories with handwritten notes. No backend, accounts, payments, or external API integrations.

The pinned direction is vintage Korean photobooth meets handmade bedroom scrapbook. All seven user-supplied references define the world: a red physical booth, dark curtain, illuminated marquee, playful controls, pastel strips, decorative stickers, pins, tape, string, and clips.
